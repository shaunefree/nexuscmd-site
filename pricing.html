Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

What happens after the free trial?

On day 15, your plan activates and your card is charged. You'll receive a reminder email 3 days before. Cancel any time in your account settings — no questions asked.

Do I need my own Anthropic API key?

No. Nexus Command™ runs Helios through our own server-side infrastructure. You do not need an Anthropic account or API key — everything is handled for you.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your conversations with Helios are processed through Anthropic's API on our servers and stored securely in our encrypted database. Your leadership profile is stored server-side and used exclusively to personalize your Helios experience. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I switch plans?

Yes, anytime. Upgrade or downgrade from your account settings. Prorated adjustments are applied automatically.

What is the annual discount?

Annual billing gives you 2 months free — an effective 17% discount. Commander goes from $468/year ($39/month × 12) to $390/year. Command Unit goes from $2,748/year ($229/month × 12) to $2,290/year.
