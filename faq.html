The FORCE Engine™ is a 10-section decision integrity protocol built into Nexus Command™. It activates automatically when a major career decision, financial investment, irreversible commitment, or strategic direction change is detected. The 10 sections are: Outside View Summary, Critic Role Declared, Core Weaknesses, Assumption Breakdown, Risk Score, Failure Scenario, Hidden Costs, Better Alternative, Final Verdict, and Confidence Rating. The final verdict is always one of: PROCEED, REVISE, or REJECT.

The Intelligence Layers
Yes. The Conflict Translator layer (CT-TE) inside Nexus Command™ is designed specifically for restaurant manager conflict situations. It activates automatically when a conflict signal is detected — crew disrespect, public confrontations, authority challenges, or hostile behavior. It acknowledges the human weight of the situation, decodes the conflict dynamics, assesses damage to authority and team trust, and gives the manager a specific corrective path with language to use.
The Accountability Assassin layer (AA-TE) inside Nexus Command™ activates when avoidance, procrastination, or failure to follow through on a leadership obligation is detected. It names the avoidance directly, states what the delay is costing, exposes the real reason behind it, issues a micro-plan for correcting it, and ends with a probe to deepen the manager's ownership of the situation. It scripts the exact hard conversation the manager has been putting off.
The Daily Debrief Manager (DDM) activates at the end of a shift when a manager describes how their day went. It produces a structured five-section output every time: Summary, Highlights, Frictions, Lessons, and Tomorrow's Anchor. It turns every shift into a structured leadership intelligence record and builds long-term growth through consistent daily practice.
The Operations Auditor (OPA) activates when operational data, performance drops, location breakdown descriptions, or metric failures are described. It produces root cause clusters ranked by operational impact, a time-bound recovery priority list, and early warning indicators. It diagnoses the specific situation — it does not offer generic best practices.
The Helios Leadership Suite™ runs a foundational intelligence layer underneath all five specialized layers. It supports operational context, equipment risk awareness, and inspection readiness across every session. It activates automatically — no selection required.
